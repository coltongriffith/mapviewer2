Step 1 of 4

Add your drill data

Export collars and assays from your drill database as CSV and drop them in. The sample project is showing until you do.

Drop CSV or ZIP files here Collars, assays, surveys, topography, or OBJ model shapes, loose or in a ZIP. Columns are matched for you.
Or paste data as text

Checklist

Collars · requiredHole ID, Easting, Northing, Elevation, Depth. Azimuth and Dip can go here too.
Assays · requiredHole ID, From, To, and grade columns (Cu %, Au g/t, CuEq…).
Surveys · optionalHole ID, Depth, Azimuth, Dip. Without it, holes run straight.
Topography · optionalX, Y, Z points or LiDAR. Without it, the surface is fitted to the collars.
Model shapes · optionalGrade shells or geology as OBJ meshes.

Coordinates in UTM metres (lat/long works for collars). Dips can be negative or positive down.
