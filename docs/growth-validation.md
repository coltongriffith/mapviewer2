# First-map validation

Prepared for the September 2026 growth release. These are proposed tests, not completed customer conversations. No outreach or ad campaign has been sent or launched.

## Run five observed sessions

Recruit five exploration professionals who have a real investor presentation or claim-monitoring task. Ask each to bring their own public claims or a file they are comfortable using. Avoid demos for this test.

1. Ask what map they need, who will use it, and how they make it today.
2. Start at the matching acquisition page below. Let them find/import their claims, choose the investor layout, and export without coaching. Record where help is needed and time to a usable export.
3. Ask whether the output could go into their next presentation and what they would change first. Record the answer verbatim in a private customer-notes system, not this public repository.
4. Ask them to save the map to a free account. Verify that the email arrives and that opening it in another tab restores the draft on the same device. Do not record login links or tokens.
5. Follow up after seven days: did they reopen or update it, use the exported map, or pay for Pro? Count an observed action separately from stated interest.

Use anonymous participant labels (P1–P5). Record: entry source, own-data import, layout selected, completed export, usable without coaching, confirmed account, saved project, seven-day return, actual payment, and the first blocking problem. Keep personally identifying notes outside GitHub.

## Decide the next investment

Proposed small-sample learning targets: four of five participants produce an output they would actually use, three confirm and save an account, and two return within seven days. These are product decisions, not statistically established conversion benchmarks. Fix repeated blockers before expanding acquisition. A larger follow-up cohort is needed to estimate conversion rates.

Then test one acquisition message at a time, with a pre-agreed spend cap if paid advertising is chosen. Evaluate cost per confirmed account and useful own-data export, subsequent return, and paid customer—not email submissions, editor opens, or demo exports alone. Review search queries and landing-page results in Search Console before commissioning more pages.
