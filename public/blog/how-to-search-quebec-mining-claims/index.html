Quebec Mining Claims Search: Find Claims by Owner, Number or Map

Search Quebec mining titles by titleholder, claim number or area below and see them on a map with holder, status, expiry date and area. It is free, needs no account, and uses Quebec's public GESTIM active-titles file, loaded weekly.

Free · no login · public registry data (refreshed extract, verify in GESTIM)

Look up a Quebec claim by number

Choose Claim number(s) and enter the title number, for example 2654321. You can paste a whole list from a claim schedule — up to 100 numbers separated by spaces, commas or new lines — and a leading "#" or "CDC" is ignored. Each number is matched exactly; for a list you are told which numbers were found and which were not. If a single number is not found, you get the titles whose numbers start with what you typed, labelled as near matches rather than presented as yours.

When a number is not found

Our file holds active titles only — including suspended titles and those pending renewal — so an expired, abandoned or revoked title will not be in it, and nor will a title registered since the last weekly refresh. The search says so and links to GESTIM, where you can check the number in the registry of record.

Look up Quebec claims by owner (titleholder)

Choose Company and type the titleholder name. Accents are optional and word order does not matter, so "Azimut Exploration" finds "Exploration Azimut inc.". If several holders match, pick the right one; their claims are then grouped by area with the claim count, total hectares and the range of expiry dates. If the full name finds nothing, the search retries with the most distinctive words and tells you it did.

GESTIM records the legal name on title, often in French and often a project subsidiary rather than the parent company. If a holder seems to have nothing, try the French form of the name or one distinctive word before concluding the ground is unheld.

Find Quebec claims in an area on the map

Choose Area and enter a latitude and longitude, for example 49.9, -74.4. The map opens on that point and loads every claim within 25 km, each holder in its own colour; you can change the radius to 10, 50 or 100 km. Leave the box empty and the map opens on Quebec: click the point you want, then Load Claims.

For anything else — work credits, renewal history, the full holder record — open the title in GESTIM.

Check a claim in GESTIM, the official registry

  1. Open GESTIM at gestim.mines.gouv.qc.ca.
  2. Use its title search to look up the title number or the titleholder.
  3. Open the title to read its current status, holder, expiry date and history.
  4. Treat GESTIM as the answer wherever it differs from a map: it is the registry of record.
Verify in the official GESTIM
Always verify current titleholder, status, expiry and boundaries in the official GESTIM before relying on the map. Exploration Maps is not affiliated with the MRNF and is not the registry of record.

How current is the Quebec data?

Quebec publishes its active mining titles as a public file, refreshed every Monday. Exploration Maps loads that file once a week and searches it, because Quebec offers no public service we can query live. A title registered, renewed or lapsed since the last load can therefore differ from GESTIM.

Data source & refresh note
Some jurisdictions are queried live; others use refreshed public datasets. Quebec uses the public file of active GESTIM titles, loaded weekly, so it can be a few days behind the official registry.

What the app can and can’t verify

Can and cannot verify
Can: find and map active titles from the public GESTIM file by holder, number or area, style and label them, and combine them with your own files.
Cannot: reflect changes since the last weekly load, or confirm legal ownership, good standing or survey-grade boundaries. Confirm specifics in GESTIM.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I look up a Quebec claim by number?
Choose Claim number(s) in the search box at the top of this page, type the title number (for example 2654321) and press Search claims. You can paste up to 100 numbers at once. If a number is not in our weekly extract of active titles, the search says so and links to GESTIM.
Can I see who owns a Quebec mining claim?
Yes. Each result shows the titleholder as recorded on title, with the status, expiry date and area. The holder is often a subsidiary and often recorded in French, so confirm ownership details in GESTIM.
How do I find all the claims a company holds in Quebec?
Choose Company and type the titleholder name. Accents and word order do not matter. Pick the matching holder and its claims are grouped by area with claim counts, hectares and expiry dates.
Is GESTIM free?
Consulting mining titles in GESTIM costs nothing. Staking a claim (map designation), renewing it and other transactions are done through a GESTIM account and carry the fees set by regulation. Searching here is also free, with no account.
How current is this data?
Quebec publishes its active titles as a public file refreshed every Monday, and we load it once a week. A title registered, renewed or lapsed since then can differ from GESTIM, which is the registry of record.
Can I search Quebec claims on a map by area?
Yes. Choose Area and enter a latitude and longitude; the map opens there with every claim within 25 km, and you can widen the radius to 50 or 100 km. Or leave the box empty and click a point on the map.
Who regulates mining claims in Quebec?
The Ministère des Ressources naturelles et des Forêts (MRNF) administers mining titles under the Mining Act. GESTIM is its system for registering and managing titles, and the registry of record.
