Manitoba Mining Claims Search: Find Claims by Number or Map

Search Manitoba mining claims by claim number or area below and see them on a map with claim name, status, good-to date and area. It is free, needs no account, and queries Manitoba's public iMaQs mining claim layer live. That layer publishes no holder names, so Manitoba has no company search.

Free · no login · public registry data (live iMaQs map layer, verify in iMaQs)

Look up a Manitoba claim by number

Choose Claim number(s) and enter the claim number as iMaQs records it, for example MB16982. Both the tenure number and the staking tag number are searched. You can paste a whole list from a claim schedule — up to 100 numbers separated by spaces, commas or new lines — and you are told which numbers were found and which were not.

When a number is not found

We search the Mining Claim layer of the public iMaQs map. Patent mining claims, mineral exploration licences, mineral leases and cancelled claims are separate layers that are not searched here. Check the number against your source, then look it up in iMaQs, the registry of record.

Why there is no company search for Manitoba

Manitoba's public claim layer publishes claim numbers, names, dates and boundaries, but not the holder. A company name has nothing to match, so the search box offers claim numbers and area only. If you type a name into the Claim number box, the search tells you so rather than returning nothing.

Finding a company’s Manitoba claims
Take the claim numbers from the company's own disclosure — news releases and technical reports often list them — and paste the list into Claim number(s). Or search the area around the project, then check each claim in iMaQs.

Find Manitoba claims in an area on the map

Choose Area and enter a latitude and longitude, for example 54.77, -101.88 near Flin Flon. The map opens on that point and loads every claim within 25 km; you can change the radius to 10, 50 or 100 km. Leave the box empty and the map opens on Manitoba: click the point you want, then Load Claims.

What you see for each Manitoba claim

FieldWhat it is
Claim numberThe iMaQs tenure number, e.g. MB16982, and the staking tag where there is one
Claim nameThe name recorded for the claim
StatusAs a code, e.g. GOOD_STAND (good standing) or PENDING
Good-to dateWhere the layer records one; a pending claim may have none
AreaIn hectares
OutlineThe claim polygon, drawn on the map
HolderNot published on the public layer

For the holder, the full record and assessment work, look the claim up in iMaQs.

Check a claim in iMaQs, the official registry

  1. Open iMaQs, Manitoba's Integrated Mining and Quarrying System, at web33.gov.mb.ca/imaqs.
  2. Use Mining Search to look up the claim number, or Mining Map to find the claim on the map.
  3. Open the claim to read its current record in the registry.
  4. Treat iMaQs as the answer wherever it differs from a map: it is the registry of record.
Verify in the official iMaQs
Always verify current holder, status, expiry and boundaries in iMaQs before relying on the map. Exploration Maps is not affiliated with the Government of Manitoba and is not the registry of record.

How current is the Manitoba data?

Each search queries the public iMaQs mining claim layer at the moment you search, so it is as current as that layer. It is the data behind the province's Mineral Dispositions map in the GIS Map Gallery. For the most up-to-date information on mineral dispositions, the province points to iMaQs or the Mines Branch.

Data source & disclaimer
Some jurisdictions are queried live; others use refreshed public datasets. Manitoba claim information comes from the public iMaQs mining map published by the Government of Manitoba and is queried live.

What the app can and can’t verify

Can and cannot verify
Can: find and map claims from the public iMaQs layer by claim number or area, style and label them, and combine them with your own files.
Cannot: show the holder, which the public layer does not publish, or confirm good standing or survey-grade boundaries. Confirm specifics in iMaQs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I look up a Manitoba mining claim by number?
Choose Claim number(s) in the search box at the top of this page, type the claim number (for example MB16982) and press Search claims. You can paste up to 100 numbers at once, and you are told which were found and which were not.
Can I see who owns a Manitoba mining claim?
Not here. Manitoba's public claim layer does not publish the holder, so our results show the claim number, name, status, good-to date and area without an owner. For the holder and current status, check the claim in iMaQs, the registry of record, or ask the Mines Branch.
How do I find all the claims a company holds in Manitoba?
Take the claim numbers from the company's news releases or technical reports and paste them into Claim number(s). Or search the area around the project and check the claims in iMaQs.
How current is this data?
Each search queries the public iMaQs mining claim layer live, so it is as current as that layer. Claim status changes, so verify anything you rely on in iMaQs, the registry of record.
Can I search Manitoba claims on a map by area?
Yes. Choose Area and enter a latitude and longitude; the map opens there with every claim within 25 km, and you can widen the radius to 50 or 100 km. Or leave the box empty and click a point on the map.
Is Exploration Maps the official Manitoba claims registry?
No. The registry of record is iMaQs, the Integrated Mining and Quarrying System of the Manitoba government's Mines Branch. Use Exploration Maps to find claims and build maps, and verify holder, status and boundaries in iMaQs.
