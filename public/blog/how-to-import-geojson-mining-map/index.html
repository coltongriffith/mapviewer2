How to Import GeoJSON Data into a Mining Map

Upload a GeoJSON or JSON file with WGS84 longitude/latitude coordinates, then assign the Claims role and style the boundary. Zipped Shapefiles and KML/KMZ can also be imported directly; conversion to GeoJSON is optional.

You do not need to convert a Shapefile or KML just to open it in Exploration Maps. Keep a copy of your original data and use the corresponding import path below.

Import and check the location

Open the upload workflow, select the file, and review the feature preview. Click Add to map, then wait for the map to fit its bounds. The sample should form a compact block in northwestern British Columbia. For your own file, compare the position with a known coordinate or landmark before styling it. Attributes remain available as feature properties.

Style the boundary and export

Assign the Claims role, choose a fill colour and border, and turn on Dissolve inner borders if you want the claim cells to read as one outline. Give the legend a meaningful name. Add a project title, source/date text, scale bar and north arrow, then check your PNG at the size it will be used.

Open the finished Claims example to compare its styling with your import. A GeoJSON file supplies geometry and attributes; it does not carry this editor layout.

Fix a missing projection or misplaced file

SymptomWhat to check
Projected Shapefile without a .prjInclude the matching .prj in the ZIP. Do not rename an unrelated projection file to make it fit.
GeoJSON has six- or seven-digit coordinatesThese may be projected metres. Re-export from the known source CRS to WGS84 before importing.
Map is in the wrong hemisphereCheck longitude/latitude order and negative signs. Western longitudes are negative.
A few features are far from the main blockInspect those coordinates and remove invalid records in a copy of the source.

When conversion is useful

Convert when a source format is unsupported, a geometry needs repair, or you want one portable GeoJSON file. In your GIS, select the correct source coordinate system and export to EPSG:4326. A projection cannot be recovered reliably from an unknown UTM zone or a guessed datum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to convert a Shapefile or KML to GeoJSON?
No. Upload a zipped Shapefile with its companion files, or upload KML/KMZ directly. Conversion is an optional preparation step.
Why does my Shapefile need a .prj?
It identifies the source coordinate system. Projected coordinates cannot be placed reliably without it. Include the original .prj or re-export in WGS84 from a known source CRS.
Does GeoJSON preserve my map styling?
Feature geometry and attributes are imported. Set colours, layer roles, labels, legend and layout in the editor; the sample layout is available separately.
