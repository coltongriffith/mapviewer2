Questions

What teams ask before they start

What data do you need from us?

Whatever you have: collar, survey and assay tables from your drill database, claim lists, geology and geophysics files, models as OBJ or DXF, and your current deck. Spreadsheets, shapefiles and PDFs are all fine. We tell you if anything is missing.

We have a geologist but no GIS person. Is that who this is for?

Yes. Your geologists keep doing the geology and sign off the technical content. We take on the drafting, styling and updating, so their time goes to the program, not to the maps.

How fast are updates?

Turnaround is agreed in your plan and set around your release schedule. Because your base maps and style are built once, a new set of results is an update, not a new project.

Who checks the figures?

We check every figure against your source data and send it to you for approval. Your Qualified Person keeps technical sign-off, and nothing is published without your approval.

Can we keep using the software ourselves?

Yes. Every plan includes Exploration Maps and the 3D maker for your team, so you can make quick figures in-house and send the important ones to us.

What if we stop drilling for a season?

Tell us and we'll pause or scale the plan back. Your base maps and style stay ready for when the program restarts.
